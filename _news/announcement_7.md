I'm delighted to share our work <a href="https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/11753442">Distributed static output-feedback approach for local consensus of Takagi-Sugeno fuzzy multi-agent systems</a>, which is my first paper published in the prestigious IEEE Transactions on Fuzzy systems! This paper presents a first-of-its-kind solution to the problem of designing distributed static output-feedback consensus protocols for nonlinear systems, providing local convergence guarantees and also a systematic framework for the estimation of the region of attraction in terms of the initial consensus error.
