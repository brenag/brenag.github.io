Very excited to share that our work <a href="https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11675994">Blind in a Changing Neighborhood: Data-Driven Distributed Output-Feedback Consensus Protocol for Multi-Agent Systems under Switching Topology</a> has been published in the IEEE Internet of Things Journal! This paper presents a unique contribution towards data-driven control techniques applied to the problem of leaderless and leader-following consensus under unreliable networks and only partial state information. By considering a polytopic representation of the graph-Laplacian spectrum, we guarantee a reduction in design complexity along with resilience against topology switching.
